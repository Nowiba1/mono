# Empire City Official Rules

## 1. Object of the Game
Empire City is a game of high-stakes municipal development and asset negotiation. The object is to acquire properties, develop complete district monopolies with villas and grand citadels, collect rent from rival developers, and force opponents into bankruptcy. The last remaining solvent player wins the title of **Grand Chancellor of Empire City**.

## 2. Setup & Preparation
- **Starting Capital**: Default \$1,500 (customizable: \$1,000 to \$2,500).
- **Turn Sequence**: Determined by turn order or random roll.
- **Bank Resources**: 32 Eco-Villas (Houses) and 12 Grand Citadels (Hotels).

## 3. Turn Flow
1. **Roll & Move**: Throw two 6-sided dice. Advance clockwise by the sum rolled.
2. **Doubles**: Rolling doubles grants an immediate extra turn. However, rolling **three consecutive doubles** immediately issues a Warrant of Arrest, sending the player to City Detention without collecting GO salary.
3. **Space Resolution**:
   - **Unowned District / Transit / Utility**: The landing player may purchase at listed price. If declined, the property goes to public auction where all players (including the decliner) may bid starting at \$10.
   - **Owned Property**: Pay rent to the owner.
     - *District*: Base rent. If the owner owns all properties in the color district (unmortgaged), base rent is doubled. Each added villa increases rent exponentially; 5 villas upgrade to 1 Grand Citadel.
     - *Transit Terminal*: Rent is \$25 (1 owned), \$50 (2 owned), \$100 (3 owned), or \$200 (all 4 owned).
     - *Utility*: $4\times$ dice roll if 1 utility is owned; $10\times$ dice roll if both utilities are owned.
   - **Vault Chest & Destiny Beacon**: Draw the top card, follow its instructions, then return it to the bottom of the deck.
   - **City Treasury Tax**: Pay \$200 to the bank.
   - **Imperial Luxury Surcharge**: Pay \$100 to the bank.
   - **City Gate (GO)**: Collect \$200 salary when passing or landing (optional double on exact landing).
   - **Free Haven**: Resting space with zero cost (optional municipal tax jackpot).
   - **Detain Order**: Move directly to City Detention.

## 4. City Detention (Jail)
A player enters detention by:
- Landing on "Detain Order".
- Drawing a "Go to Detention" card.
- Rolling three consecutive doubles in one turn.

While in detention, a player **still collects rent** on owned properties. A player may leave detention by:
1. Paying the \$50 municipal fine before rolling.
2. Using a "Pardon Pass" (Get Out of Detention Free card).
3. Rolling doubles on their turn (up to 3 attempts). If doubles are not rolled by turn 3, the player must pay \$50 and move by the roll.

## 5. Development & Even-Building Rule
- You must own all properties in a color district to build.
- You must build **evenly**: you cannot build a second villa on any property in a district until all properties in that district have at least one villa.
- 4 villas are exchanged for 1 Grand Citadel (Hotel).
- Selling buildings back to the bank returns 50% of their purchase price, also following the even-breakdown rule.

## 6. Mortgages
- Any unencumbered property without buildings can be mortgaged to the bank for 50% of its face value.
- Mortgaged properties collect zero rent.
- To unmortgage, pay the mortgage value plus 10% municipal interest to the bank.

## 7. Trading
- Players may trade cash, properties, and pardon passes on any turn.
- A property cannot be traded if any buildings exist in its district; all buildings must be sold back to the bank first.
- Mortgaged properties can be traded; the recipient must either pay the 10% interest immediately or lift the mortgage.

## 8. Bankruptcy
- When a player owes more money than their cash plus maximum liquidation value (cash + 50% building values + mortgage values of all properties), they must declare bankruptcy.
- All remaining assets are transferred to the creditor. The bankrupt player is eliminated.
